02 / Extension Architectures

Extensibility across ArtCraft apps.

PHOTOCRAFT

WASM Filters & LUTs

Sandboxed WebAssembly pixel-manipulation modules (ABI v1) and 3D LUT cubes for non-destructive color grading, sharpening, and stylization.

EFFECTCRAFT

WASM Effects & ScriptUI

Sandboxed WebAssembly effect plugins (API v1) for rendering, paired with dockable ScriptUI / ExtendScript (.jsx) panels for animation, easing, and captioning.

VECTORCRAFT

Object Filters & Live Effects

Sandboxed WebAssembly plugins running in wasmi with fuel metering for procedural path transforms and appearance effects, plus SVG template sets.

FILMCRAFT

Effect Presets & LUTs

Native timeline effect presets (Lumetri color, grain chains) in JSON format plus 17/33/65-cube .cube files for video color grading.

SOUNDCRAFT

CLAP, VST3 & Audio Units

Native host for open CLAP (.clap) and VST3 (.vst3) audio plugins (and AU on macOS), plus mix engine channel strip DSP presets.

PDFCRAFT

Acrobat JavaScript & Actions

Sandboxed Acrobat JavaScript (ISO 32000 in pure-Rust Boa engine) for interactive form calculations, field validation, and document automation.

DESIGNCRAFT

Templates & Layout Styles

Structured IDML publication templates, text import filters, and typographic layout presets.

LIGHTCRAFT

RAW Presets & Profiles

Camera develop parameter presets and 3D LUT profiles for non-destructive RAW photo development.

CADCRAFT

Automation Scripts & Macros

Command automation scripts and parametric drawing macros for drafting and 2D/3D geometry.

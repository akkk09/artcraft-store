# Attribution and Clean-Room Statement

Citron is an original, clean-room implementation inspired by the concept of 3D DCC-style graph editors (such as Autodesk Maya's Graph Editor, Blender's Graph Editor, and the aescripts Citron tool by Philippe Duvin).

- All mathematical solvers, de Casteljau Bézier subdivision logic, Free-Form Deformation (FFD) lattice cage algorithms, normalization pipelines, and ScriptUI panel code were written from scratch by EffectCraft contributors under MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- No proprietary code, binaries, decompiled assets, or presets were inspected, referenced, or included.
